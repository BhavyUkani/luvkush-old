Keep your account safe

  • Use a unique password

    Avoid reusing a password from another site — a mix of letters, numbers and symbols is strongest.

  • Never share your password

    Luv Kush Natural will never ask for your password over email, call, or WhatsApp.

  • Sign out on shared devices

    If you've logged in on a public or shared computer, always sign out when you're done.

Danger zone

Permanently delete your account and all associated data — orders, addresses, and wishlist. This action cannot be undone.
