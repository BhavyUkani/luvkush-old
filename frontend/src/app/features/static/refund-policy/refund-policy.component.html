Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your order, here's exactly how we can help — and how quickly.

7 days Return window
5–7 days Refund processing
48 hours To report damage

Eligible for return

  • Requested within 7 days of delivery
  • Unused, unopened, in original packaging
  • Defective items, regardless of sale type

Not returnable

  • Opened or used hair care products (hygiene)
  • Custom-made wigs or hair patches
  • Digital downloads
  • Clearance / flash-sale items, unless defective

How to initiate a return

  1. 1 Contact us at support@luvkushnatural.com or on WhatsApp with your order number and reason for return.
  2. 2 We'll confirm eligibility and arrange a free pickup — no extra cost for eligible returns.
  3. 3 Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund is processed within 5–7 business days.

Refund processing

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving and inspecting the returned item. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are sent via bank transfer.

Received a damaged or incorrect item? Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos, and we'll replace or refund it at no charge.
