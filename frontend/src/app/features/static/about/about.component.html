Our story

From our grandmothers' kitchen, to your home

Luv Kush Natural was born from a simple truth: the most effective hair care formulas were already in our grandmothers' kitchens. Cold-pressed amla, slow-infused bhringraj, fresh-ground neem — these weren't exotic treatments. They were rituals.

We founded Luv Kush Natural to honour those rituals and make them accessible to every Indian household. Our oils and treatments are formulated exactly as they would have been centuries ago — without shortcuts, synthetic preservatives, or marketing gimmicks.

Our mission
“To make authentic natural and herbal hair care accessible to every household — with transparency in ingredients, honesty in claims, and a commitment to natural beauty.”
