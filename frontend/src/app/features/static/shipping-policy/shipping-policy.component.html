Shipping charges

We offer free standard shipping on all orders above ₹499, anywhere in India.

Orders below ₹499
₹49 flat shipping fee
Express delivery
₹99 regardless of order value

Delivery timelines

Metro cities

3–5 business days, standard delivery.

Tier 2 / Tier 3 cities

5–7 business days, standard delivery.

Remote areas

7–10 business days.

Order processing

Orders are processed Monday–Saturday, 10 AM – 5 PM IST. Orders placed after 5 PM or on Sundays are processed the next business day.

Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an SMS and email with the tracking link. You can also track it anytime from the My Orders section.

If a package is returned due to an incorrect address or repeated failed delivery attempts, we'll contact you to reschedule. Reshipping charges may apply.
